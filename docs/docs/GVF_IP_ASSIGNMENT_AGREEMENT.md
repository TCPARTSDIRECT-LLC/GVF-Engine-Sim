INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT 
This Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into effective as of September 4, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between: 
• 	Assignor: TCPartsDirect, LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("Assignor"), and • 	Assignee: GVF Dynamics, LLC, a Florida limited liability company (Document No. 
L26000442442), whose sole Authorized Member is TCPartsDirect, LLC ("Assignee"). 
RECITALS 
WHEREAS, Assignor is the sole owner of all right, title, and interest in and to certain intellectual property, inventions, and technical assets relating to hardware resource governance, dynamic thresholding, and sub-cycle bitline gating architectures, including United States Provisional Patent Application Serial No. 64/134,522 (the "Patent Rights"); and 
WHEREAS, Assignee is a subsidiary operating entity of Assignor, organized under the laws of the State of Florida; and 
WHEREAS, Assignor desires to assign, transfer, and convey all of its worldwide right, title, and interest in and to the Patent Rights and associated hardware artifacts to Assignee for commercialization, licensing, and management purposes. 
AGREEMENT 
1.	Assignment of Intellectual Property. 
Assignor hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers, and conveys to Assignee, its successors and assigns, all of Assignor’s worldwide right, title, and interest in and to: 
•	(a) United States Provisional Patent Application No. 64/134,522, filed with the USPTO and entitled "Global Voltage & Frequency / Gated Vector Field Governance Engine" (the "Provisional Application"), together with any and all non-provisional, divisional, continuation, continuation-in-part, reexamination, reissue, extension, or foreign priority applications deriving therefrom; 
•	(b) The GVF Engine™ hardware IP architecture, including synthesizable SystemVerilog 
RTL implementation files (gvf_core_top.sv, gvf_bitline_comparator.sv), testbench suites, simulation scripts, whitepapers, and PyTorch/snnTorch algorithmic models; 
•	(c) All common law, unregistered, and registered trademark rights in GVF Engine™ and associated branding; and 
•	(d) All claims, causes of action, and rights to sue or recover for past, present, or future infringement or misappropriation thereof. 
2.	Consideration. 
This assignment is made in consideration of Ten Dollars ($10.00) cash in hand paid and other good and valuable consideration, including the capital structuring of Assignee, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged by Assignor. 
3.	Representations and Warranties. 
Assignor represents and warrants that: 
•	(a) It is the sole and unencumbered owner of the assigned Intellectual Property; 
•	(b) It has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement; and 
•	(c) The assigned Intellectual Property is free and clear of all liens, mortgages, security interests, licenses, or encumbrances of any kind. 
4.	Further Assurances & USPTO Recordation. 
Assignor agrees to execute and deliver such further instruments, including formal recordation documents for the USPTO Electronic Patent Assignment System (EPAS), as Assignee may reasonably request to perfect Assignee's title and record ownership in the assigned Intellectual Property.
